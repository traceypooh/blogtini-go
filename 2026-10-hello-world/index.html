It's `2026-10-hello-world/index.html` -- open it, and see: it's just some settings at the top, then markdown. To write another post: 1. Copy this folder, w/ a new name -- eg: `2026-11-my-trip/` (dates first keep them in order). 2. Change the `title:` & `date:` up top, & write. 3. Pictures go in `img/`. Use one at the top w/ `featured:`, or anywhere in your post, eg: `![my dog](../img/dog.jpg)` 4. Push -- your site updates in a minute or two. Some markdown: - *italics*, **bold**, [a link](https://blogtini.com/docs/) - lists, like this one - `code`, & quotes: > Keep the highlights of your life in plain text files -- in a free git repo, that lives forever. Try a different look: change `theme:` in `config.yml` -- or see your site in every theme, at your `/preview/`.
